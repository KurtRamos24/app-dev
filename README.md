# My Favorite Movie: Miracle in Cell No. 7

## Introduction

**Miracle in Cell No. 7** is one of my favorite South Korean drama movies. It tells the heartbreaking story of a father with an intellectual disability who is wrongly accused of a crime and separated from his beloved daughter.

---

## About the Movie

The story follows *Lee Yong-gu*, a loving father who lives with his young daughter, Ye-sung. After being falsely accused of murder, he is sent to prison and placed in Cell No. 7. His cellmates eventually discover his kindness and help him reunite with his daughter.

### Main Characters

- **Lee Yong-gu** – A loving father who cares deeply for his daughter.
- **Ye-sung** – Yong-gu's intelligent and loving daughter.
- **Yang-ho** – The leader of the prisoners in Cell No. 7.
- **The Cellmates** – Prisoners who become Yong-gu's friends and help him.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because:

1. It shows the unconditional love between a father and daughter.
2. It teaches the importance of family and friendship.
3. It has emotional and unforgettable scenes.
4. It reminds us not to judge people based on their appearance or condition.

> A father's love will always remain strong, no matter what challenges he faces.

## My Favorite Thing About the Movie

My favorite part is the relationship between Yong-gu and Ye-sung. Even though they experience many difficult situations, their love for each other never disappears. The movie shows that being a good parent is not about being perfect but about giving unconditional love to your child.
